What if your next brand ambassador wasn't human?

AI influencers are already attracting audiences and landing brand deals. Fast Company profiled Aitana Lopez, a virtual creator with hundreds of thousands of followers.

New tools can also let businesses create a digital character and place it into product videos.

That gives marketers another way to test creative ideas without filming every version from scratch.

But attention doesn't guarantee sales. And a fictional character presented as a real customer can mislead viewers.

The smart move? Use AI characters to test ideas—and be transparent about what people are watching.
